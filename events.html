Riverbend Yoga Events

At Riverbend, we offer monthly workshops for all types of students. Come join us!

Monthly Workshops

  • Beginner Foundations: First Saturday of each month, 10 a.m. to 11 a.m. Practice basic poses and breathing with Cloud Strife.
  • Intermediate: Second Saturday of each month, 1 p.m. to 2 p.m. Push yourself to the next level with Aerith Gainsborough.
  • Advanced: Third Saturday of each month, 4 p.m. to 5 p.m. Hone your craft and push your skillset with Tifa Lockheart.
