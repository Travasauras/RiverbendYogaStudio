Welcome to Riverbend Yoga Studio

Our Mission

Here at Riverbend Yoga Studio, we aim to make yoga accessible to everyone. We want to limber you up and bring you closer to your inner light.

Beginner Friendly

Whether it's your first day or you're a seasoned stretcher, we have classes for everyone! Riverbend Yoga Studio is a judgment-free zone where everyone is welcome.

Featured Class: Gentle Yoga

Our Gentle Yoga class is designed for beginners and those looking for a slower-paced yoga experience. The class focuses on simple poses, stretching, and relaxation.

Class Schedule

Riverbend Yoga Studio offers a variety of classes throughout the week. Whether you prefer a gentle introduction or a more active yoga session, we have options for different experience levels. Classes are one hour and start at the top of every hour from 10AM - 6PM Monday through Saturday.
