About Riverbend Yoga Studio

Riverbend Yoga Studio is a welcoming space to slow down, stretch, and take time for yourself. Whether you are new to yoga or returning to your practice, we encourage you to move at your own pace.

Meet Our Instructors

Accessibility Accommodations

We understand that while not everyone has the same capabilities everyone deserves to be accommodated. At Riverbend we try our best to accommodate anyone regardless of ability by providing:

  • Chairs, blocks, and straps are for support.
  • Breaks or pose skips are okay. Don't overdo it.
  • Instructors explain movements step by step.
  • Anyone who makes this inclusive space feel non-inclusive will be asked to leave.

Studio Policies

  • Please arrive 10 minutes before class.
  • Silence phones.
  • Respect each student's space, experience, and comfort level.
  • Instructors must ask permission before offering hands-on assistance.
  • If you need to cancel a reserved or private class, please let us know in advance.
